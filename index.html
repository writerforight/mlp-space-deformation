Quick guide
  1. Look: press ▶ under the view — objects and the grid go through every layer (linear step, then activation).
  2. Inspect: open ◫ Network, click a layer — the right panel shows its weights, activation and history. Double-click a weight to change it.
  3. Train: Training → pick a task, press ★ Recommended, then ▶ Train.
  4. Topology: open Topology experiments to see what an invertible network cannot do — and what one extra dimension changes.
